Simple by design

One less thing to remember.

Bins Out is being built to make bin day easier for busy homes, carers looking after family, and property managers juggling several addresses.

🏠

Keep homes separate

Save more than one household on the same device and switch between addresses without overwriting the other home's details.

📅

Bring dates to your calendar

Where collection dates are confirmed, export calendar events with an evening-before alert and import them into your phone calendar.

🔎

Check before you trust

Collection lookup support varies by council. Bins Out tells you when dates still need confirming and points you back to official council sources.

Important: council-data coverage is still being expanded. Always confirm that a returned schedule matches your exact property. The app does not currently send its own independent background push notifications; calendar alerts are handled by your calendar app.
